Jacksonville Beach

Jacksonville Beach is a lively coastal community known for its wide sandy shore, iconic fishing pier, and bustling beachfront parks. Surfers and sunbathers flock here for steady Atlantic swells and plenty of space to spread out. The adjacent Atlantic Boulevard corridor offers shops, casual cafés, and a vibrant nightlife scene under the glow of string lights and tiki torches.

St. Augustine Beach

Just south of Jacksonville, St. Augustine Beach captures a mix of historic charm and seaside relaxation. The beach’s shoreline is softer and gentler, perfect for families with young children. After a day of building sandcastles, visitors can explore nearby St. Augustine’s centuries-old historic district, where Spanish colonial architecture and ghost-tour legends come alive.

North Beach & Guana River State Park

North Beach, tucked within Guana River State Park, offers one of the region’s most pristine and undeveloped stretches of coastline. Protected marshes and maritime hammock forests back the dunes, providing habitat for migratory birds and sea turtles. Kayaking the river estuary or hiking the park’s nature trails gives a true sense of northeastern Florida’s raw beauty.

Atlantic Beach

Atlantic Beach seamlessly combines boutique shops, farm-to-table eateries, and a laid-back surf culture. The coastline here is consistently ranked clean and family-friendly, with lifeguarded zones and gentle waves. On any given day you’ll spot paddleboarders gliding past the palm-fringed dunes, while local artists display handmade crafts at the beachfront park.

Neptune Beach

Neptune Beach, just one mile east of Mayport, is celebrated for its community-oriented vibe and annual spring festival. Its compact downtown—often called “Neptune Village”—is packed with ice cream shops, surf stores, and live-music venues within easy walking distance of the sand. Whether you’re catching early-morning surf or sunset drum circles, Neptune Beach embodies true Floridian coastal living.
