Integration of STEM and Leisure Technologies

The article by Singh & Srivastava discusses the significant increase in social media usage in India, particularly highlighting its influence on travel decisions. It notes that travelers increasingly rely on social media for reviews, travel modes, and accommodation preferences, making it a crucial part of their decision-making process.

Social Media’s Influence on Travel Planning

Social media is a game-changer when it comes to travel decisions, especially for Indian travelers, as Singh and Srivastava pointed out. Platforms like Instagram, Facebook, and YouTube are packed with visual content, reviews, and recommendations that really influence where people decide to go. This isn’t just an Indian thing—it’s happening all over the world. People love sharing their travel experiences, which inspires others and provides tons of useful info about destinations, places to stay, and things to do.

From my own experience, I have not been very present on social media recently and even before I wouldn’t say it had an impact on my travel decisions. However other STEM technologies have made an impact on my planning and coordination travel. When it comes to planning, whomever I am going on the trip with or going to visit or usually in an iOS messaging group chat for easy communication. I have downloaded both the American Airlines and Spirit Airlines app for traveling before. The apps helped with adding my boarding pass to my Apple Wallet, tracking flight times or delays, and keeping track of any connection flights I’d have.

Technology Acceptance and Ease of Use

The acceptance and ease of use of these technologies are crucial factors driving their adoption in travel planning. According to the Technology Acceptance Model (TAM), perceived ease of use and perceived usefulness significantly impact users' attitudes toward technology (Davis, 1989). As these technologies become more intuitive and integrated into daily life, their adoption in travel planning has increased. For instance, the rise of mobile apps and responsive websites has made it easier for travelers to access and use these tools on the go, contributing to their widespread acceptance. Interestingly, technology can sometimes lead to unexpected situations. For example, a 100-year-old traveler named Mildred Kirschenbaum was recently mistaken for an unaccompanied minor due to a glitch in the airlines’ computer systems. The system only allowed for two digits in the birth year field, so her birth year of 1923 was entered as ‘23’, causing the mix-up. This highlights how technology, while incredibly useful, can sometimes have quirky and unforeseen consequences.

The Impact of Technology Failures on Leisure Activities

Despite the benefits, technological failures can significantly impact leisure activities. The recent worldwide tech outage on July 19, 2024, caused by a routine software update from CrowdStrike, led to widespread disruptions across various sectors globally. Airlines like American Airlines, Delta, and United experienced severe delays and cancellations, causing chaos at airports (NBC News, 2024).

Banks in Australia and New Zealand faced difficulties, with customers unable to access their accounts (USA Today, 2024). Retail businesses like McDonald’s in Japan and Waitrose in the UK had to close some stores or switch to cash-only transactions (MSN, 2024). Public transit systems in the U.S. also experienced temporary disruptions (USA Today, 2024).

These incidents highlight the need for robust backup systems and contingency plans to mitigate the impact of technology failures. Continuous monitoring and testing of software updates are crucial to prevent similar issues in the future.
