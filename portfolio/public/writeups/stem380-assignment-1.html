Subject: Balancing Artificial Intelligence

As artificial intelligence (AI) becomes increasingly integrated into our daily lives, it presents

both economic opportunities and ethical challenges. Societal perspectives on AI can vary

significantly. On one hand, there’s a viewpoint that fully embraces technological optimization

and economic benefits. On the other hand, some prioritize transparency, accountability, and the

preservation of human judgment in AI decision-making processes. For my paper, we’ll cover the advantages and disadvantages of AI, considering both economic gains and ethical

implications.
