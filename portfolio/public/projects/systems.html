ENTD268 — Information System Design · INFO498 — Information Technology: Capstone

From requirements to system design

Data flows, application architecture, and a point-of-sale capstone.

Objectives and implementation

ENTD268 — Information System Design develops requirements through data flow diagrams, a class model, an entity relationship model, and application architecture. INFO498 — Information Technology: Capstone presents a point-of-sale design with a network topology, screen layout, cost breakdown, and project schedule.

Verified evidence

The retained diagrams and design artifacts demonstrate a requirements-to-design process. They are academic designs, not evidence of deployed infrastructure or measured availability.

Limitations

Implementation, user acceptance, capacity, cost accuracy, and operational performance were not independently validated. These diagrams describe academic systems; they do not disclose employer infrastructure.
