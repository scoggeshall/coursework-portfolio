Jacksonville Beach

Jacksonville Beach has a little bit of everything, from the long fishing pier to plenty of local restaurants and a big stretch of open sand. It’s a popular spot whether you want to surf, fish, or just hang out by the water.

St. Augustine Beach

St. Augustine Beach feels more relaxed and historic. The area has an old-Florida vibe with the pier, small shops, and a laid-back atmosphere that makes it great for a slower pace.

Guana River State Park

Guana River State Park, often called North Beach, is about as quiet and undeveloped as it gets around here. If you want wide beaches with fewer crowds, this is the place to go.

Atlantic Beach

Atlantic Beach has a neighborhood feel with local cafes, small shops, and a community that really values its surf and outdoor lifestyle. It’s easy to spend a whole afternoon here.

Neptune Beach

Neptune Beach is right next to Atlantic Beach and has a lot of the same laid-back energy. It’s known for its friendly local events and the spring festival that draws a good crowd every year.
