Human Relations Communication · Week 8

Week 8

Slide 1 — Resolving Conflict and Dealing With Difficult People in the Workplace

  • HRMT101 B001: Human Relations

Slide 2 — Introduction & Objectives

  • Purpose of Training
  • Recognize the importance of proactively addressing workplace conflict and difficult behaviors
  • Learn strategies and programs for creating a healthier, more respectful culture
  • Learning Objectives
  • Identify negative impacts of unchecked conflict
  • Understand proactive measures to mitigate conflict and negative behaviors
  • Develop a plan for ongoing conflict resolution training and accountability

Slide 3 — Unchecked Conflict (1 of 2)

  • Low Morale & Decreased Productivity
  • Ongoing tension creates stress and drains focus
  • Higher Turnover & Absenteeism
  • Skilled employees leave toxic environments
  • Stress-related illnesses or avoidance of conflict-ridden teams
  • Damaged Team Dynamics
  • Lack of trust, collaboration, and open communication

Slide 4 — Unchecked Conflict (2 of 2)

  • Reputation & Legal Risks
  • Escalated conflicts can lead to harassment or discrimination claims
  • Negative public perception if issues go public
  • Opportunity Cost
  • Time and energy spent on putting out fires instead of on growth and innovation
  • Long-Term Organizational Impact
  • Erodes organizational culture and undermines mission success

Slide 5 — Positive Measures

  • Focus Areas:
  • Proactive Training & Development
  • Clear Policies & HRM Support
  • Early Intervention Systems
  • Leadership Accountability
  • Building a Positive Workplace Culture
  • New Training Programs & Processes

Slide 6 — Proactive Conflict Resolution Training

  • Workshops & Role-Plays
  • Teach active listening, negotiation, de-escalation
  • Peer Mediation Sessions
  • Trained “peer mediators” to facilitate early conflict resolution
  • Regular Refreshers
  • Annual or semiannual sessions keep skills sharp
  • Real-World Scenarios
  • Practice with case studies reflecting actual workplace challenges

Slide 7 — Clear Policies & HRM Support

  • Formal Conflict Resolution Policy
  • Outlines steps: informal dialogue → mediation → escalation
  • Zero-Tolerance for Harassment/Bullying
  • Communicates non-negotiable standards of respectful conduct
  • Open-Door & Whistleblower Protections
  • Encourage employees to raise concerns without fear of retaliation
  • HR as Facilitator
  • Confidential coaching and guidance for both employees and supervisors

Slide 8 — Early Intervention Systems

  • Regular Manager Check-Ins
  • Frequent one-on-ones to detect issues early
  • Tracking Indicators
  • Monitor turnover, absenteeism, grievance frequency
  • Anonymous Reporting Channels
  • Online forms, hotlines, or suggestion boxes
  • Employee Pulse Surveys
  • Short surveys to gauge morale and identify potential conflict zones

Slide 9 — Leadership Accountability

  • Performance Evaluations
  • Integrate conflict resolution goals into supervisors’ performance appraisals
  • Manager Coaching
  • Provide training on difficult conversations and negotiation techniques
  • Lead by Example
  • Model respectful and calm behavior under pressure
  • Recognition & Rewards
  • Incentivize effective conflict resolution among teams and leaders

Slide 10 — Building a Positive Workplace Culture

  • Team-Building Activities
  • Foster trust, mutual understanding, and collaboration
  • Diversity & Inclusion Programs
  • Emphasize respect for differences to reduce bias-based conflicts
  • Wellness Initiatives
  • Stress management, mental health resources, Employee Assistance Programs (EAPs)
  • Transparent Communication
  • Frequent updates and open dialogue to reduce rumors and mistrust

Slide 11 — Proposed New Training & Processes

  • 1. Conflict Resolution Certification Path
  • Tiered modules (basic → advanced), practice-based evaluations
  • 2. Performance-Based Accountability
  • Tie managerial conflict resolution success to compensation/promotion
  • 3. Peer Support Networks
  • Ambassadors or “conflict coaches” within teams
  • 4. Ongoing Learning & Refresher Courses
  • Micro-learning sessions on empathy, active listening, and respect
  • 5. Expected Outcomes
  • Improved team harmony, reduced turnover, enhanced reputation
