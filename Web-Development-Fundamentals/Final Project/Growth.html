Different fields

Cybersecurity connects to the rest of IT

The field of Information Technology can branch in many directions. Cloud computing alone connects with identity, networks, storage, monitoring, application security, compliance, and operations.

That range is why an Information Security Analyst role can be a strong launch point. It teaches how systems connect, where risk appears, and how technical teams communicate when something is at stake.

Cloud

Identity and access

Learn how users, devices, and applications authenticate and receive permission.

Networks

Traffic and defense

Understand segmentation, firewalls, VPNs, routing, and network monitoring.

Operations

Response and recovery

Practice incident response, backups, documentation, and communication during events.
