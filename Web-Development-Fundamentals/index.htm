Breaking Down Kanye Wests Music

Kanye West, now formally and legally known as Ye, has had an insane career filled with some ups and downs. Throughout
this article I am going to break down some of my favorite moments. From best vereses, crazy samples, and stories that he has
shared with the world.

Sample City

One of my favorite parts of Kanyes discography is his consistent usage of samples ranging from old soul music, to modern techno
EDM. My Personal favorites are Gyongyhaju Lany by Omega used on New Slaves, Sermon (He'll Give Us What We Really Need) by Holy Name
of Mary Choral Family used in On Sight, and Try a Little Tenderness by Otis Redding in the song Otis.

Regarding the song New Slaves, Kanye was actually sued for using the sample. He never had gotten it cleared from the owner
before using it in his song. The sample is used for over a minute and half for the outro of the track with vocals from Frank
Ocean layered in. Gabor Presser, the Hungarian composer for "Gyongyhaju Lany" (The Girl with Pearly Hair), found out about
the usage after New Slaves was released. He originally sued for 2.5 million in copyright claims but he and Ye settled the issue
out of court for an undisclosed amount.

Intro Style

Kanye used "On Sight" as the intro to the albulm Yeezus. He said that he had thought of using a more subtle song to start but
he really wanted to let the listener know that he was frusturated. From the first sound of "On Sight" you can feel his anger through
the music. In the past albulms he starts off with soft vocals and beats to slowly get you into the albulm but Ye said this albulm
was me for him than it was for the listeners. When Yeezus was initially released it got alot of back lash, people saying Kanye is
to full of himself and just genuinely not enjoying the sound. ALthough this was the case, the albulm has grown into a fan faovrite.
